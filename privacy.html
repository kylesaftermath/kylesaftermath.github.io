Privacy Policy

Aftermath Studio · Last updated: October 11, 2026

Aftermath Studio ("the App", "we", "us") is a private, internal automation tool operated by Kyle Mullaney (Aftermath Essentials) via kylesaftermath.github.io. It is used only by its owner to manage creative project files and review YouTube channel performance. It is not offered to the public.

1. Information we access

When the owner signs in with Google, the App requests access to the following Google user data:

  • Google Drive — to create folders and upload, organize and download project files (scripts, storyboards, video renders).
  • Google Sheets — to create and update spreadsheets such as project cost logs.
  • Google Docs — to create and edit documents such as scripts and briefs.
  • YouTube (read-only) — to read channel, video and comment information.
  • YouTube Analytics (read-only) — to read channel performance metrics such as views, watch time and retention.

The App does not access Gmail, Calendar, Contacts or any other Google service.

2. How we use information

Google user data is used solely to provide the App's features to its owner: storing and organizing creative assets, maintaining cost logs, and producing performance reports and content recommendations. We do not use Google user data for advertising, and we do not sell, rent or trade it.

3. Sharing

We do not share Google user data with third parties, except:

  • with AI model providers used to process content at the owner's request (for example, summarizing analytics or drafting documents), under their data-processing terms and without use for advertising;
  • when required by law.

4. Storage and security

OAuth access tokens are stored on a private, access-restricted server and are never published or shared. Files remain in the owner's own Google Drive. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, after which the App can no longer access any Google data.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep Google user data only as long as needed to perform the requested task. Revoking access deletes the App's ability to access data; locally cached copies are deleted on request or when no longer needed. To request deletion, contact us below.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy

Aftermath Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.

9. Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/kylesaftermath/kylesaftermath.github.io
